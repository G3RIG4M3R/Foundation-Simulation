# Scope and modeling limitations

The proposed simulation blends the early Foundation's religious and commercial
influence into an abstract period. It is not a historical chronology or an
empirically calibrated account of Asimov's universe. Anacreon, Smyrno, Konom,
and Daribow are literary inspiration; their numeric profiles are assumptions.

Planets will be stationary turtles in toroidal space. The intended population
is Terminus, four groups of five kingdom planets, and ten independent worlds.
Independent worlds do not share a government. A tick is an abstract interval.
The model excludes war, conquest fleets, psychohistorical predictions, and
scripted surrender. Influence is reversible and distinct from legal annexation.

Religion and economic dependency will be separate state variables. Proposed
wealth gains represent net development benefits, not double-entry accounting.
Initial traveling agents are free; later replacement costs reduce net trade
profit. Inventory and upkeep are abstracted. These simplifications must remain
visible when interpreting future results.

No model behavior or experimental outcome has yet been validated. Parameter
ranges, thresholds, and the proposed 450-tick horizon need empirical checks
within the simulation before any scientific conclusion is drawn.
